# Rothletach

To the people of Corinth, the sun is their everything. On Torr which has two suns, solar eclipses are frequent, occurring once every 7 years. Generally the larger sun passes in front of the smaller one, causing a reduction in brightness and a reddening of the sky. In a religious context, at this time, Cor and his son Encor are said to be in perfect unity of will. It is a time of great prosperity and a good omen for business contracts, marriages, and large purchases. This is referred to as _Rothletach_, the Day of Red Light.

# Dolnetach

Roughly once a century, however, a phenomenon the people of Corinth refer to as **Dolnetach**, the Darkest Day, occurs. The smaller sun passes in front of the larger one, almost completely blacking it out. Religious citizens believe Encor becomes jealous of his father's power, and battles him to seize control of the heavens. The sky turns violet and the streets fall silent. The veils between worlds are said to be thinned at this time, and any manner of insidious creature could make its way onto the mortal plane to wreak havoc. Until Encor defeats his father, assuming his power and his name, the sky remains dark. People often remain in solitude during this time, with some laying salt in front of their doors and windows for protection.
