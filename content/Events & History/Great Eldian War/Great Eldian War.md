The [[Corinthian|Corinthians]] made landfall at [[Port Caine]] in 503 GG. Their explorations into the ruins of an ancient human society on Orun and the relics they found were their primary impetus to "take back the land".

The Humans advancd their settlements westward, establishing the town of [[Greenbridge]] on the Northeastern shores of the [[White River]] in 504 GG. The land of the river valley near Greenbridge was among the sought-after fertile soil of notable scarcity in the harder northern lands.

Nomadic [[Lycanthropes|Lycanthrope]] herders dependent on wild rye and the animals that grazed it forged a [[Name of the Treatise|treatise]] with the humans at the town's founding to share the land's resources. The peace did not hold long.
