# Twin Suns

The planet [[Torr|Torr]] resides in a binary star system. The residents of [[Places/Orun/Eldia|Eldia]] & [[Corinth - Nation|Corinth]]
refer to these two stars as [[Sola]], the Greater Sun, and [[Ensola]], the Lesser Sun. The two suns orbit a shared barycenter in a binary orbit. Each planet in the system has a circumbinary orbit around the same barycenter.
