# [[Madam Valentine|Madam Valentine]]

The [[Campaign/Heroes/Heroes|party]] visited the proprietor of the [[Pearl Lounge]] in [[Session 4]], where [[Meeka|Meeka]] saved the evening from a dreadful stage performance by [[Libera]]. Madam Valentine gave the party [Tarot readings](https://readings_link.com) and recommended they leave [[People/z_Minor/Millicent of Goldleaf|Milli]] with the [[Daughters of Ilovia]].

# [[Rowena Rumblefoot]]

Rowena hosts both [[Ash|Ash]] and [[Meeka|Meeka]] in her [[Rowena’s Cottage|cottage]], though Ash has been there for much longer. Last she saw the party in [[Session 5]], she gave the [[Campaign/Heroes/Heroes|party]] grave news regarding Ash’s condition.
